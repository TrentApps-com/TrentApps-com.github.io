Our Own Product hiroi

An assistant that answers your customers when you can't.

hiroi Assistant is the one product we build and run ourselves. It goes on your site in a single line of code, learns your menu, your hours, your policies and your documents, and then handles the questions you'd otherwise answer at 9pm — by text or out loud.

  • Trained on your material. Upload the menu, the price sheet, the FAQ. It answers from those, not from the open internet.
  • Voice or text. The same assistant that types on your website can talk on the phone.
  • It knows when to stop. Anything it shouldn't decide alone gets handed to a human, with the whole conversation attached.
  • It drops into any of the examples above. Ordering, booking, intake — the assistant sits on top of the system we built you.
