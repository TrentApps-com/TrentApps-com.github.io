Chapter II · The Flagship

hiroi Assistant

An AI assistant that lives on any website in one script tag — voice and text, aware of the page it's on, trained on your documents, answering customers the moment they reach out.

One script tag

Drops into any site. No SDK, no build step, no backend changes — paste it and the assistant is live.

Voice and text

A natural voice customers actually talk to — streaming speech, noise cancellation, live waveform — with rich text chat beside it.

Page-aware + RAG

Reads the page it's on, highlights what it's talking about, and answers from your own uploaded documents.

Human handoff

Books work, takes orders, guides tours — and hands off to a real person the moment one is needed.

Live · Beta Real Customers

This site is the demo — Maya, in the corner, is hiroi.

She reads every message to maya@trentapps.com, answers visitors out loud, and never sleeps. Built end to end by one person: the voice pipeline, the RAG stack, the dashboard, the billing.
