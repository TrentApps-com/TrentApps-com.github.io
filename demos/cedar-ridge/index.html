What we do

Four kinds of work, one crew

Every job gets walked before a machine moves. What follows is what we actually charge for — the estimator further down uses these same numbers.

Brush & overgrowth clearing

Reclaiming pasture, yard edges and old fields that have gone to cedar and blackberry. Cut low, hauled or chipped, ground left workable.

from $950 / acre

Invasive species removal

Bush honeysuckle, autumn olive, callery pear and English ivy — cut, treated and followed up the next season so it doesn't come back.

from $1,250 / acre

Fenceline reclamation

Six to ten feet cleared either side, posts exposed, and the line left straight enough to run new wire the same week.

from $4.50 / linear ft

Forestry mulching

One machine, one pass, no burn pile. Best on standing brush under six inches where you want the material left as ground cover.

from $1,400 / acre

Haul-off & debris

Loaded and gone the same day, or stacked where you want it. Priced by the load, not by the guess.

from $385 / load

Follow-up season

Cleared ground grows back. We come back the following spring and treat the regrowth before it's brush again.

from $340 / acre
