Working Examples

Six businesses that don't exist, running on software that does

Each one is a full build you can click all the way through — place the order and watch it hit the kitchen screen, book the chair, receive the shipment, price the job.

These are demonstration builds, not client work. The businesses are invented so we can show the whole system — the storefront and the back office — without exposing anyone's real numbers. Yours would carry your name, your menu, your prices, your workflow.
