The part most shops get wrong

A booking page is only useful if it tells the truth about who's actually free. Ironside's does — it holds the slot the second you tap it, and it knows that a straight-razor shave takes forty minutes, not fifteen.

  • Real availability. Each barber has their own hours and days off. The grid only offers what's genuinely open.
  • Service-aware timing. A 75-minute appointment blocks 75 minutes. Nobody stacks a shave on top of a fade.
  • No app, no account. Name and phone number. That's the whole form.
  • Reminders that reduce no-shows. A text the night before and the morning of, with a one-tap cancel that frees the chair.
