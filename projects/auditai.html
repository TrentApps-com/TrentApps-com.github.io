Capabilities

Features

Risk Classification Engine

Guided wizard classifies AI systems into four EU AI Act risk tiers: unacceptable, high, limited, and minimal. Stores classification rationale for audit purposes.

Compliance Checklists

Interactive checklists mapped to real regulatory requirements across 10 categories including risk management, data governance, transparency, and human oversight.

Gap Analysis Matrix

Color-coded matrix showing compliance status across every system and requirement category at a glance. Instantly identify where your organization is falling short.

Deadline Countdown

Tracks upcoming regulatory deadlines like the Colorado AI Act (Feb 2026) with countdown timers and overall compliance scoring against each framework.

AI System Inventory

Central registry of every AI system in your organization. Track vendor, department, deployment status, risk level, and compliance progress in one place.

Audit Trail

Every action logged with timestamps, user attribution, and system context. Tamper-evident trail that satisfies regulatory requirements for record-keeping.
