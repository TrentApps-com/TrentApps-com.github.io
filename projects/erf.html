ERF — Live Earth

Real-time planetary data on an interactive 3D globe

A photorealistic 3D globe with real-time earthquake tracking, wildfire monitoring, ocean temperature heatmaps, city lights, and country borders. An AI voice interface enables hands-free queries about global data. Layer controls and time-range filtering let you explore datasets across the entire planet.

Demo

Explore the interactive 3D globe — toggling real-time earthquake data, wildfire monitoring, ocean temperature overlays, and using the AI voice interface to query global datasets.

Capabilities

Features

Real-Time Earthquake Tracking

Live seismic data plotted on the globe with magnitude-scaled markers. Filter by time range, view depth data, and track aftershock patterns.

Wildfire Monitoring

Active fire data from satellite sources displayed as heat points on the globe. Track fire progression, intensity, and affected regions.

Ocean Temperature Heatmaps

Sea surface temperature data rendered as color-coded overlays. Visualize thermal patterns, currents, and anomalies across the world's oceans.

AI Voice Interface

Ask questions about global data using natural language. “Show me earthquakes in Japan this week” or “What’s the ocean temperature near Hawaii?” — the globe responds.

City Lights & Borders

Toggle photorealistic city light overlays and country border data. See urbanization patterns and geopolitical boundaries rendered on the 3D surface.

Layer Controls

Intuitive controls to toggle data layers on and off, adjust time ranges, filter by magnitude or intensity, and customize the visual presentation.
